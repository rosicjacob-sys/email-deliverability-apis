0Executive Summary: What Actually Happened to Our Sales?

The logical reconciliation between our store orders, Deep2K channel numbers, and Gerry's ad spend sheet.

Bottom-Line Finding: Our sales did not appear out of thin air from people randomly typing city URLs into their browser. When we cross-reference Gerry’s daily spend log against Deep2K’s store-level traffic, campaign tables, and source code, four facts are proven beyond doubt:
  1. Google Ads sent real traffic AND real orders to the exact city stores Gerry paid for — but the Channel Summary hid them: In Oct 20–26, Gerry spent $412 for 1,034 Google Ads clicks across 9 Canadian city stores. Deep2K’s Store Table (Section 4) literally lists Paid Search as the #1 Top Channel on vaughanpeps.ca (567 visitors, +734%, 2 paid orders / CA$179.60) and pickeringpeps.com (65 visitors, 1 paid order / CA$76.10). Yet Deep2K’s Channel Summary (Section 2) reported 0 orders and $0.00 revenue for Paid Search!
  2. Why Paid Search orders were misfiled under Direct (36% of orders) or (unattributed):
    • Broken Checkout Handoff / Missing Snippets: When a customer clicks a Google Ad and goes to checkout, if the storefront runs an older v1 tracker (like oshawapeptides.store), is missing the checkout snippet (like pickeringpeps.com after its theme switch), or the handoff token lapses, the order arrives at Deep2K without a linked session_id.
    • Multi-Session Buying Lag (People Don't Buy on Click #1 in Peptide E-Commerce): Researching peptides takes days. A buyer clicks Gerry’s Google Ad or TikTok Ad on Day 1, reads COAs/dosages, leaves, and comes back on Day 3 or Day 6 by typing the domain or tapping an open mobile tab. If localStorage/cookie state was cleared (or in iOS/TikTok in-app browser → Safari switch), Session #2 starts as Direct.
    • First-Touch Default (model=first): Every Deep2K report defaults to model=first. Any customer whose very first visit weeks ago was untagged/Direct or Organic Search, and who later clicked Gerry's Google Ad to actually buy, has 100% of their order credited to their old first touch and $0 credited to Paid Search.
  3. Why Deep2K says "no ad spend in Deep2K yet, so no ROAS": Deep2K V3 already has a built-in Google Ads Spend Feed endpoint (POST /api/spend-feeds/ingest) and a ready-to-paste Google Ads MCC script (apps/api/src/lib/googleAdsScript.ts), but nobody installed the script in Gerry's Google Ads MCC, AND Gerry’s campaign names don't follow Deep2K’s required <city>_<YYYY-MM-DD> naming rule (packages/shared/src/campaignNaming.ts). As a result, Google Ads spend in Deep2K is $0, numeric campaign IDs (24353743836, 24360315351, 24365570351 = 574 sessions) remain unresolved numbers, and ROAS cannot be calculated.
  4. Why Montreal (montrealpeptides.com) had 27 "Direct" orders (CA$3,188.34) even after visitors dropped 66%: Between Oct 1 and Oct 19, Gerry pumped $317 and 4,053 TikTok Ad clicks into Montreal (plus $352 / 2,553 clicks into Toronto and $333 / 4,185 clicks into Winnipeg = 10,791 TikTok clicks). On Oct 20, Gerry cut Montreal TikTok Ads to $0. Traffic immediately dropped 66% (1,539 → 526 visitors), but paid orders jumped from 17 to 27 (CA$3,188.34) — almost all credited to Direct + promo emails (montreal-free-ghk-cu, pc-new-coas-dropped). Those were delayed conversions from the Oct 1–19 TikTok wave + repeat buyers!

2The Store Table vs. Channel Table Contradiction Explained

How a store's #1 Top Channel can be Paid Search with paid orders, while the Channel Summary reports 0 orders for Paid Search.

Look at apps/api/src/routes/dashboard.ts (lines 95–103 & 195–215):
In Deep2K’s code, a store’s topChannel column is calculated from visitor traffic (ft, the most common channel among visitors to that store), whereas the Channel Summary’s Paid orders column is calculated from the orders.first_channel column on the order row (channelOf(model, 'o'))!

Here is what happens step-by-step on vaughanpeps.ca and pickeringpeps.com:

  1. Gerry sends 470 Google Ads clicks to vaughanpeps.ca (?gclid=... or ?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=24353743836).
  2. Deep2K’s Cloudflare Worker receives those 567 visitors and classifies their sessions as Paid Search. Because 500+ of Vaughan's 567 visitors are Paid Search, Vaughan’s Top channel in Section 4 is displayed as Paid Search.
  3. Now 2 of those customers actually buy on vaughanpeps.ca (spending CA$179.60) and 1 buys on pickeringpeps.com (spending CA$76.10).
  4. Why didn't those 3 orders get first_channel = 'Paid Search' on the orders row?
    • Path A (Shared Checkout Portal): When the buyer clicks Checkout, the storefront script tries to call POST /api/sites/:id/handoff. If the store's checkout_domain isn't configured, or the theme navigated before the 2500ms token fetch completed, the order arrives at POST /api/integrations/checkout/events without a handoff_token (recordWithoutStore at integrations.ts:255). The order is saved with first_channel = NULL ((unattributed)), or if recoverAttribution (attributionRecovery.ts) matches an earlier untagged visit by the same customer, it copies Direct!
    • Path B (Shopify Webhook): When the order arrives via POST /api/sites/:id/webhook/shopify (webhooks.ts:206), Deep2K ONLY checks two things: (1) cart note_attributes written by order-attribution-capture.deep2k.liquid (which is not installed on most stores, or as Carl reported, was lost on pickeringpeps.com during its theme switch!), and (2) Shopify's order.landing_site / order.referring_site. Crucially, webhooks.ts NEVER joins the Shopify order against journey_sessions! And if the customer came back in a second session (typed URL / bookmark) when placing the order, Shopify's landing_site is / with referring_site = "", which parseAttribution('/', '', site.domain) classifies as Direct!

3The Buying Cycle & Lag Effect (Why Ads Look Like "Direct" Days Later)

Why peptide buyers rarely convert on their first ad click, and how our current tracking erases the ad that introduced them.

Case Study A: Montreal TikTok Wave (Oct 1–19 → Oct 20–26)
  • Oct 1–19: Gerry spends $317 on TikTok Ads for Montreal, generating 4,053 clicks (A2-Montreal).
  • Oct 13–19: montrealpeptides.com has 1,539 visitors and 17 paid orders (CA$2,388.73).
  • Oct 20–26: Gerry pauses Montreal TikTok Ads ($0 spend). Paid Social visits drop 98.6% (1,051 → 15). Total Montreal visitors drop 65.8% (1,539 → 526).
  • The Lag Conversion Surge: Despite 66% fewer visitors, Montreal paid orders jump +59% from 17 to 27 orders (CA$3,188.34), with a 5.13% conversion rate!
  • Why it showed as Direct: TikTok ads open inside TikTok's in-app WebView (BytedanceWebview). When a buyer later opens Safari/Chrome to purchase, or taps an SMS/bookmark days later, Safari does not share localStorage (d2k_aid_v1) with TikTok's WebView! So their purchase session has a brand-new anonymous_id and first_channel = Direct.

  • Waterloo (waterloopeps.ca): Gerry spent $206 (222 clicks) in Oct 9–19 → 119 visitors and 3 paid orders (CA$510.31). When paused in Oct 20–26 ($0), visitors dropped to 33 and orders dropped to 0.

5Mandatory Google Ads & TikTok Ads UTM Specification

Every single ad MUST carry both an Account-Level Tracking Template and a Campaign Custom Parameter (`{_campaign}`) so zero clicks ever arrive untagged.

Rule #1 for Gerry (Ads Manager): Never rely on raw Final URLs alone, and never put `{campaignid}` inside the Final URL field! In Google Ads, ValueTrack macros like `{campaignid}`, `{adgroupid}`, `{network}`, `{creative}`, and `{targetid}` are guaranteed to expand inside the Tracking Template, while the human-readable campaign name (`city_YYYY-MM-DD_offer`) is passed via a Custom Parameter (`{_campaign}`) on each campaign.

5.1 Google Ads Account-Level Tracking Template (Set Once per Ad Account)

In every Google Ads account (or at the MCC level): go to Settings → Account Settings → Tracking → Tracking template and paste this exact string:

{lpurl}?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={_campaign}&utm_id={campaignid}&utm_content={adgroupid}_{creative}&utm_term={keyword}&network={network}&placement={placement}&matchtype={matchtype}&targetid={targetid}&loc_physical_ms={loc_physical_ms}
  • Why this works with packages/shared/src/attribution.ts:
    • utm_source=google & utm_medium=cpc: Immediately classifies as Paid Search (or if network=d → Paid Display; if network=ytv → Paid Video; if network=x → Paid Shopping via googleNetworkChannel() at attribution.ts:183!).
    • utm_campaign={_campaign}: Passes the human-readable campaign name in Deep2K’s mandatory format: <city>_<YYYY-MM-DD>_<offer> (e.g., vaughan_2026-11-03_search).

/**
 * Deep2K: send daily campaign spend, impressions & clicks to Deep2K.
 * Paste in Google Ads → Tools → Bulk actions → Scripts (MCC or individual account).
 * First run: set DAYS = 90 to backfill October. Then set DAYS = 7 and Schedule: Daily.

var DEEP2K_URL = "https://new-deep2k.onrender.com/api/spend-feeds/ingest";
var DEEP2K_KEY = "PASTE_D2K_SPEND_KEY_FROM_DASHBOARD";
var DAYS = 90;

function main() {
  var accounts = [];
  try {
    var it = AdsManagerApp.accounts().get();
    while (it.hasNext()) accounts.push(it.next());
  } catch (e) {
    accounts = null; // Single account mode
  }
  if (accounts === null) {
    send([collect()]);
    return;
  }
  var batch = [];
  for (var i = 0; i < accounts.length; i++) {
    AdsManagerApp.select(accounts[i]);
    try { batch.push(collect()); } catch (e) { Logger.log('Skipped ' + accounts[i].getCustomerId() + ': ' + e); }
    if (batch.length === 10) { send(batch); batch = []; }
  }
  if (batch.length) send(batch);
}

function collect() {
  var account = AdsApp.currentAccount();
  var tz = account.getTimeZone();
  var day = 24 * 3600 * 1000;
  var to = Utilities.formatDate(new Date(Date.now() - day), tz, 'yyyy-MM-dd');
  var from = Utilities.formatDate(new Date(Date.now() - DAYS * day), tz, 'yyyy-MM-dd');
  var rows = [];
  var report = AdsApp.search(
    'SELECT segments.date, campaign.id, campaign.name, campaign.advertising_channel_type, ' +
    'metrics.cost_micros, metrics.impressions, metrics.clicks FROM campaign ' +
    "WHERE segments.date BETWEEN '" + from + "' AND '" + to + "' AND metrics.impressions > 0");
  while (report.hasNext()) {
    var r = report.next();
    rows.push({
      date: r.segments.date,
      campaign_id: String(r.campaign.id),
      campaign: r.campaign.name,
      campaign_type: r.campaign.advertisingChannelType,
      cost: Number(r.metrics.costMicros || 0) / 1000000,
      impressions: Number(r.metrics.impressions || 0),
      clicks: Number(r.metrics.clicks || 0)
    });
  }
  return { id: account.getCustomerId(), name: account.getName(), currency: account.getCurrencyCode(), rows: rows };
}

function send(accounts) {

    method: 'post',
    contentType: 'application/json',
    headers: { 'X-Deep2K-Key': DEEP2K_KEY },

    muteHttpExceptions: true

  Logger.log('Deep2K ' + res.getResponseCode() + ': ' + res.getContentText());
  if (res.getResponseCode() >= 300) throw new Error('Deep2K refused the spend: ' + res.getContentText());
}

8Buying-Cycle & Conversion-Lag Telemetry Specification

How we measure the exact hours and days between a customer seeing/clicking an ad (`first_seen_at` / `last_non_direct_at`), submitting an order (`submitted_at`), and paying (`paid_at`).

The Business Question We Must Answer Every Day:
"When Gerry spends $100 on Google Ads or TikTok Ads today, how many hours or days does it take for those people to actually buy, how many visits do they make before buying, and how much of our 'Direct' revenue is actually delayed conversions from ads clicked 2–14 days ago?"

8.1 The 4 Timestamps Already in Deep2K’s Database (and the 2 Missing Links)

Look at packages/db/src/schema.ts in new-deep2k:

  • anonymous_visitors.first_seen_at: The exact timestamp the user first landed on the store (e.g. clicked a TikTok or Google Ad on Oct 12).
  • anonymous_visitors.last_non_direct_at: The exact timestamp of their most recent non-Direct visit (e.g. clicked a Google Ad on Oct 21).
  • orders.submitted_at: The exact timestamp the order was placed.
  • orders.paid_at: The exact timestamp the e-transfer/payment was confirmed.

What is missing today:

  1. On `orders`, we do not store `first_touch_at` or `last_non_direct_at` when copying attribution from `anonymous_visitors` or `order-attribution-capture.deep2k.liquid` (`attr_captured_at` / `attr_last_captured_at`).
  2. In `apps/api/src/routes/salesReport.ts`, we only compute `AVG(EXTRACT(epoch FROM (o.paid_at - o.submitted_at)) / 3600)` (`hoursToPay`), never `hoursFromFirstTouchToOrder` or `hoursFromLastAdToOrder`!

8.2 Ready-to-Run SQL Query: Buying-Cycle Lag & Multi-Touch Distribution

Add this query to apps/api/src/routes/salesReport.ts (inside `settleAll` as `buyingCycle`) so `/v1/analytics/sales` and the Dashboard Sales page display the full buying-cycle breakdown per channel:

-- Add to apps/api/src/routes/salesReport.ts inside settleAll({...})
buyingCycle: db.execute(sql`
  WITH order_lag AS (
    SELECT
      o.id,
      o.site_id,
      ${channelOf(model, 'o')} AS channel,
      COALESCE(o.currency, 'UNK') AS currency,
      ${NET()} AS amount,
      o.submitted_at,
      o.paid_at,
      -- Earliest known touch timestamp (visitor first_seen_at, or first session prior to order)
      COALESCE(
        av.first_seen_at,
        (SELECT MIN(js2.started_at) FROM journey_sessions js2
         WHERE js2.anonymous_id = o.anonymous_id AND js2.started_at <= o.submitted_at),
        sj.started_at
      ) AS first_touch_at,
      -- Latest non-direct touch timestamp before the order
      COALESCE(
        av.last_non_direct_at,
        (SELECT MAX(js3.started_at) FROM journey_sessions js3
         WHERE js3.anonymous_id = o.anonymous_id
           AND js3.started_at <= o.submitted_at
           AND js3.first_channel IS NOT NULL
           AND js3.first_channel <> 'Direct')
      ) AS last_ad_touch_at,
      -- Total sessions by this visitor before buying
      COALESCE(
        (SELECT COUNT(*)::int FROM journey_sessions js4
         WHERE js4.anonymous_id = o.anonymous_id AND js4.started_at <= o.submitted_at),
        1
      ) AS sessions_before_order
    FROM orders o
    LEFT JOIN anonymous_visitors av ON av.id = o.anonymous_id
    LEFT JOIN journey_sessions sj ON sj.id = o.session_id
    WHERE ${ordersPaid(from, to)}
  )
  SELECT
    channel,
    COUNT(*)::int AS paid_orders,
    ROUND(AVG(EXTRACT(epoch FROM (submitted_at - first_touch_at)) / 3600.0)::numeric, 1)::float AS avg_hours_first_touch_to_order,
    ROUND(AVG(EXTRACT(epoch FROM (submitted_at - last_ad_touch_at)) / 3600.0)::numeric, 1)::float AS avg_hours_last_touch_to_order,
    ROUND(AVG(sessions_before_order)::numeric, 2)::float AS avg_sessions_to_order,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE submitted_at - first_touch_at < interval '1 hour')::int AS bucket_under_1h,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE submitted_at - first_touch_at >= interval '1 hour' AND submitted_at - first_touch_at < interval '24 hours')::int AS bucket_1h_to_24h,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE submitted_at - first_touch_at >= interval '1 day' AND submitted_at - first_touch_at < interval '3 days')::int AS bucket_1d_to_3d,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE submitted_at - first_touch_at >= interval '3 days' AND submitted_at - first_touch_at < interval '7 days')::int AS bucket_3d_to_7d,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE submitted_at - first_touch_at >= interval '7 days' AND submitted_at - first_touch_at < interval '14 days')::int AS bucket_7d_to_14d,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE submitted_at - first_touch_at >= interval '14 days')::int AS bucket_over_14d,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE sessions_before_order > 1)::int AS multi_session_orders
  FROM order_lag
  GROUP BY 1
  ORDER BY paid_orders DESC
`)
